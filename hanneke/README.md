# Hanneke

An atmospheric, colourful experience built on the work of Hanneke de Neve, a
Dutch-born artist in New Jersey and a friend of the repository owner: her
specific pieces, the colour of her fibercollages, quilts and paintings, and
the fluid line of her drawings for the interface.

So far this folder holds the reference material only. Nothing is published
yet; a `publish.sh`, a Pages workflow step and a card in the game library come
with the first playable build.

- [`reference/`](reference/README.md): 33 photos of 28 of her works and two of
  her (fibre and textile, paintings, drawings and collage), a catalogue with
  sources, her statement, sampled palettes and style notes on colour and line.
  Reference only, never published.
- [`intake/`](intake/README.md): drop box for new photos of her work and
  anything else for this project.
