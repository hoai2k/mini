How to play

Every stick, trigger and button moves something in the picture: some a little, a few a lot. Try them all.

Small moves set up big ones. Bring a piece to the middle, or sew pieces together, and a big control reaches further.

Nothing to win. Leave it be and the picture settles. Stuck? Pause and choose the eye.
