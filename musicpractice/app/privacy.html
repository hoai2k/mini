Music Practice: privacy and terms

Applies to the Music Practice website and its iPhone, iPad and Android apps. Last updated 20 October 2026.

Privacy

Music Practice doesn't collect your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The one thing that leaves your device is feedback you choose to send (below).

  • Microphone. The tuner and the Playback Recorder listen through the microphone. The sound is analysed on your device and never sent anywhere.
  • Recordings. Takes are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They leave it only when you save or share one yourself. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data) deletes them.
  • Settings (tempo, theme, tuner and drone choices) are stored only on your device.
  • Purchases (the apps). Music Practice Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play, which handle payment under their own privacy policies. The app uses RevenueCat to check whether Pro is on: RevenueCat receives the purchase record and an anonymous ID made by the app, not your name, email or payment details (RevenueCat's privacy policy).
  • Fonts. The apps carry their fonts with them. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your device's IP address when it serves them (Google's privacy policy).

Feedback

Send feedback (in Settings, and Report a problem in Audio check) sends only what you write and press Send for:

  • Your email address, if you give one (it is optional), so the developer can reply. It isn't used for anything else, added to any list, or shared.
  • Your message, and the kind of feedback you picked.
  • Device details, if you leave them on (you can see exactly what they are before sending): whether it's the website or an app, the screen size, the theme, the app's settings for the metronome, tuner and drone, and what the microphone and sound output reported in Audio check. Also what your browser says about itself: its name and version, language, time zone and screen size.
  • Debugging data, only if you tick Include debugging data (it starts unticked every time): the most recent entries of the app's debug log, described below.

The debug log. To help track down problems that only show on a particular phone, the app keeps a log on your device of what its sound and microphone did lately: when sound started and stopped and how loud it came out, the audio engine's states, when the microphone was opened or closed and why, and the names of the microphones and speakers your device reports (a Bluetooth headset's name, say). It holds no recordings, nothing you said or played, and nothing you typed. It stays on your device (the newest 1,500 entries) and is sent only if you include it in feedback, or export it from Audio check with Export debug data and send the file yourself.

It goes to a private Google Sheet belonging to the developer, through Google Apps Script (Google's privacy policy), and the developer gets an email saying it arrived. It is kept only as long as it's useful for answering you and fixing what you reported, and at most two years, then deleted. To have something you sent deleted sooner, ask through the support page (or in reply to an answer).

Music Practice is not directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone. If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.

Questions about privacy, or a request to see or delete what you sent: use the support page.
