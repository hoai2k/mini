Music Practice support

Help with the Music Practice website and its iPhone, iPad and Android apps.

Contact the developer

Write here, or in the app use Settings › Send feedback (it can include your device's audio details, which help with sound problems). Leave your email if you'd like a reply. What you send is handled as the privacy policy describes.

Common questions

How do I get Pro back on a new phone, or after reinstalling?

Open Settings › Get Pro… and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription or the free trial?

iPhone and iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Music Practice Pro. Android: Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before the trial or year ends to avoid the next charge. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple or Google, who handle refunds: on Apple devices at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android through Google Play's order history.

I bought Pro on iPhone. Does it work on Android?

No. The app has no accounts, so a purchase belongs to the store it was made in. It does carry over to your other devices on the same store.

The tuner or recorder doesn't hear anything

The app needs the microphone. If you said no when it asked, turn it on in the phone's settings (iPhone: Settings › Music Practice › Microphone; Android: Settings › Apps › Music Practice › Permissions). Audio check, in each tool's settings, shows what the app can hear.

How do I delete my data?

Everything you make (takes, settings) is stored only on your device: deleting the app deletes it. To have feedback you sent deleted, ask using the form above.
