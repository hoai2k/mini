# Drone artwork for Music Practice

Prepared images for a future Drone feature: sustained cello tones to play along with.
Artwork only; no Drone functionality, home button, screen or runtime asset entries are implemented.

One transparent cello-and-bow home-button emblem per theme, with sound ribbons suggesting a sustained tone.
Exports match the other home-button images: 384×384 WebP with alpha. Labels will be supplied by the app.
