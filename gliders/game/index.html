      How to glide

      Objective. Score the most points in the round. Everyone flies the same sky at the same time, one view each.

      Fly close. Flying near any block fills your combo: the closer and faster, the faster it fills. Your contrails glow and the surface lights up in your colour while it counts.

      Tricks raise the multiplier (+1 each) while you are close: Thread a gap with blocks on both sides, fly a Tunnel, Skim a surface very close, fly Upside down, go at Blazing speed, or keep the Flow going for six seconds.

      Bank it. Fly clear of everything for two seconds and the combo times the multiplier is banked into your score. Touch a block first and the whole combo is lost. Dive into the clouds below or fly off the edge and you are sent back to the start, combo banked.

      Scoring modes. Best run (as in Superflight): a crash ends your run and you start again from the top; your score is your best run of the round. Score attack: everything you bank counts; a crash only loses the open combo.

      Flying. Dive to gain speed; climbing spends it, and too slow you stall and the nose drops. Bank to turn, and pull up while banked to turn tighter. Tuck in to dive faster; flare out to brake and float.

      Joining. On the glider screen each player presses A on their own controller (or Space / Enter on the keyboard) to join. Then ◀ ▶ picks a glider, ▲ ▼ its colours, Y (F or \ on the keyboard) inverts the nose controls, and A again is ready. B steps back. Up to four players.

      Pitch follows a plane by default: pull the stick back to pull the nose up. Invert it per player on the glider screen. Flight assist (Settings) gently levels the wings when you let go of the stick.
