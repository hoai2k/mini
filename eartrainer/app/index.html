How to play

A melody plays. Play it back on the keyboard, note by note.

  • The glowing dot marks the first note. It disappears once you've played it.
  • Wrong note? Keep trying: the melody waits for the right one. Wrong notes cost stars and points; timing never matters.
  • Repeat plays the melody again. Redo starts your answer over from the first note.
  • Practice lets you try notes freely without answering. Turn it off to carry on where you were.
  • Speed slows the melody down or speeds it up.
  • Stars: three for a clean answer, two for a few slips, one for the rest. Clean answers in a row build a streak bonus.
  • In Adaptive level, melodies get longer and harder as you do well, and easier when they're tough.
