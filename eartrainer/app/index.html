How to play

Hear a melody, then play it back on the keys.

  • The glowing dot marks the first note. Wrong notes don't stop you: keep trying until you find the right one. Timing doesn't matter.
  • Play it once with no wrong notes to finish it: three stars on the first try, two on the second, one after that.
  • Repeat plays it again. Redo starts over. Practice lets you try notes freely. Tempo slows it down or speeds it up.
  • Tap a note on the staff to hear it. Drag the little piano above the keys to move along the keyboard.
  • On the choice screen, pick a songbook and a difficulty, then Melody Challenges, Full Songs (a whole song, phrase by phrase), Focus Mode (works on what you find hardest), Your Ears (what you hear well) or Free Play.
