# Percy Jackson: how Percy and Annabeth have been shown

This page collects the ways Percy Jackson, Annabeth Chase and their world have
been drawn, painted, filmed and staged since *The Lightning Thief* (2005). Use it
to pick an art direction for a possible game. Each section below is one style
family. It shows a few examples and describes how that family draws the two
heroes, its palette and line, and how well it would work in a game.

- **[Open the gallery](index.html)** to see all 72 images with filters and a
  search.
- [`manifest.json`](manifest.json) records the credit, year, source URL and
  style notes for every image.
- [`images/`](images/) holds the files, resized to at most 1600 px.

All images belong to their credited owners (Rick Riordan's publishers, the
artists, 20th Century Fox, Disney, the musical's producers, Activision). They
are kept here as private reference only. They are not published with the games
and must not be used as game assets. A game should get its own art that borrows
a *direction*, not the pictures.

## The constants

Almost every version agrees on a short list of identifiers. Any game style
needs to keep them:

- **Percy:** dark, messy hair; sea-green eyes (the books are firm on this, while
  films and covers vary); orange Camp Half-Blood T-shirt or a hoodie and jeans;
  Riptide, a bronze sword that is a ballpoint pen when capped; water and
  lightning around him.
- **Annabeth:** curly blonde hair in the books (Leah Sava Jeffries plays her
  with braids in the Disney+ series); grey eyes; orange camp shirt; a bronze
  knife; a Yankees cap that makes her invisible; a bead necklace; a Greek
  breastplate and plumed helmet when in battle.
- **The world:** Camp Half-Blood's orange shirts, bronze armour, the trident,
  lightning, Greek pottery motifs and a modern New York skyline.

## 1. Painted adventure (John Rocco)

<img src="images/rocco-us-cover-lightning-thief.jpg" width="160" alt="Rocco's Lightning Thief cover"> <img src="images/illustrated-edition-original-trio.jpg" width="200" alt="Grover, Percy and Annabeth"> <img src="images/illustrated-edition-annabeth-percy-infirmary.jpg" width="200" alt="Annabeth tending Percy"> <img src="images/illustrated-edition-lightning-thief-cover.jpg" width="160" alt="Illustrated Edition cover">

These are the original US covers (2005–2009). Rocco also illustrated
*Percy Jackson's Greek Gods* (2014), *Greek Heroes* (2015) and *The Lightning
Thief Illustrated Edition* (2018). The covers are dark, backlit oil-style
paintings: Percy is small and seen from behind, with lightning and sea spray
behind him. The Illustrated Edition interiors are different. They are soft
watercolour vignettes with loose edges, ordinary-looking kids in orange shirts,
and a gentle, humorous tone. Annabeth wears a blonde ponytail and has an
expressive face.

The Brazilian Intrínseca cover and the Taiwanese edition use the same kind of
painted storm-and-sea drama.

- **For a game:** The interior watercolour style suits a 2D story game: painted
  backdrops, character vignettes, scenes that ease in and out of white. The
  covers are mood paintings for title screens rather than a way to draw
  characters.
- **Watch for:** painterly art costs more per frame. It works best with few,
  large poses rather than lots of animation.

## 2. Painted portraits (*The Demigod Files*)

<img src="images/demigod-files-caparo-annabeth.jpg" width="200" alt="Annabeth tipping her Yankees cap"> <img src="images/demigod-files-caparo-percy.jpg" width="200" alt="Percy with Riptide">

Antonio Caparo's portraits for *The Demigod Files* (2009) are warm, glossy
digital paintings with soft light and slightly stylised young faces. Annabeth
tips her Yankees cap with a sly grin, in the camp shirt, with a ponytail. Percy
holds Riptide upright. These portraits are the most "official" faces in the
book world.

- **For a game:** Well suited to dialogue portraits beside a simpler in-game
  sprite or model, as in visual novels and RPGs.
- **Watch for:** only a handful of characters were drawn, so the rest of the
  cast would need to be invented in this style.

## 3. Graphic novels

<img src="images/graphic-novel-lightning-thief-cover.jpg" width="150" alt="Graphic novel Lightning Thief cover"> <img src="images/graphic-novel-interior-som-annabeth-percy-sirens.jpg" width="170" alt="Annabeth at the helm, Percy at the mast"> <img src="images/graphic-novel-interior-annabeth-panel.jpg" width="220" alt="Annabeth close-up panel"> <img src="images/graphic-novel-last-olympian-cover.jpg" width="150" alt="Last Olympian graphic novel cover">

Robert Venditti adapted the novels and Attila Futaki drew them (*Lightning
Thief* 2010, *Sea of Monsters* 2013, *Titan's Curse* 2013), coloured by José
Villarrubia. The art is realistically proportioned, inked and painted, with
moody lamplight and indigo night scenes. Annabeth has long straight blonde hair
and older-teen features. *The Battle of the Labyrinth* (2018) and *The Last Olympian* (2019), by Orpheus
Collar and Antoine Dodé, lean toward anime: big eyes, green irises and graphic lighting.

- **For a game:** a natural fit for comic-panel cutscenes between levels. The
  Collar/Dodé cover is a bridge between realistic and anime.
- **Watch for:** the Futaki style is detailed and realistic, which is hard to
  keep consistent at small sprite sizes.

## 4. UK Puffin action composites

<img src="images/uk-puffin-2013-cover-lightning-thief.jpg" width="150" alt="Puffin 2013 Lightning Thief"> <img src="images/uk-puffin-2013-cover-titans-curse.jpg" width="150" alt="Puffin 2013 Titan's Curse"> <img src="images/uk-puffin-2009-cover-last-olympian.jpg" width="150" alt="Puffin 2009 Last Olympian">

The 2013 UK covers use photographic or photoreal digital composites. A teenage
Percy is caught mid-action against fiery orange or electric blue. The original
UK *Last Olympian* is glowing blue and magenta.

- **For a game:** these are only references for lighting and action poses in a
  realistic 3D game. They aren't a style that can be drawn.
- **Watch for:** Annabeth barely appears in this family.

## 5. Ornamental flat (Victo Ngai and the redesigns)

<img src="images/lightning-thief-us-2022-redesign-cover.jpg" width="150" alt="US redesign Lightning Thief"> <img src="images/us-2022-redesign-cover-titans-curse.jpg" width="150" alt="US redesign Titan's Curse"> <img src="images/chalice-of-the-gods-us-cover.jpg" width="150" alt="Chalice of the Gods US cover"> <img src="images/wrath-of-triple-goddess-cover-victo-ngai.jpg" width="150" alt="Wrath of the Triple Goddess"> <img src="images/lightning-thief-french-albin-michel-collector-cover.jpg" width="150" alt="French collector cover">

Victo Ngai painted the covers of the new books (*Chalice of the Gods* 2023,
*Wrath of the Triple Goddess* 2024). The redesigned US paperbacks of the first
five books are in the same style, and so is the current Italian edition, which
reuses that art. The paperback credits weren't confirmed against the books
themselves; see the manifest. The covers are emblems rather than scenes:
Riptide, the trident, a Pegasus, a kylix or a Hecate mask. They use flat,
decorative shapes, art-nouveau line, Greek-vase friezes and jade, coral, gold
and teal grounds. People appear only as tiny vase-painting figures. The French
collector edition's gold-framed pastel throne room fits the same decorative
feeling.

- **For a game:** excellent for menus, the title screen, maps, loading screens,
  item icons and the world (temples, sea, sky). Flat shapes are cheap to make
  and scale cleanly. It could be paired with a character style from families 2,
  3 or 6.
- **Watch for:** there's no model for Percy's or Annabeth's faces in this
  family.

## 6. Bright YA cartoon

<img src="images/lightning-thief-spanish-salamandra-2023-cover.jpg" width="170" alt="Spanish 2023 Lightning Thief cover"> <img src="images/chalice-of-the-gods-spanish-salamandra-cover.jpg" width="170" alt="Spanish Chalice of the Gods cover"> <img src="images/chalice-of-the-gods-uk-puffin-cover.jpg" width="170" alt="UK Chalice of the Gods cover">

The Spanish Salamandra covers (2023) are clean, cel-shaded and anime-adjacent,
with expressive poses. They are the most Annabeth-forward images in the set.
On the Lightning Thief cover she's blonde and grinning, throwing her cap, with
Percy, Grover and Poseidon beside her. On the Chalice cover the trio surround
the cup. Khadijah Khatib's UK *Chalice* cover is related but neon and graphic:
a flat leaping Percy under rainbow light, among snakes.

- **For a game:** probably the easiest family to turn into a readable 2D cast.
  It has clear silhouettes, flat colour that animates well, an appealing
  12-year-old tone and a full trio. It works for side-scrolling, top-down or
  visual novels.
- **Watch for:** it can drift toward generic anime unless Greek motifs from
  family 5 anchor it.

## 7. Shadow-play silhouette (Japan)

<img src="images/japan-holp-lightning-thief-cover.jpg" width="160" alt="Japanese Lightning Thief cover"> <img src="images/japan-holp-titans-curse-cover.jpg" width="160" alt="Japanese Titan's Curse cover"> <img src="images/japan-demigod-files-sword-of-hades-cover.jpg" width="160" alt="Japanese Demigod Files cover">

The Japanese Holp Shuppan editions use black cut-paper silhouettes on layered
grey and navy, with gold foil type. Percy is an angular shadow with spiky hair
and a sword. The covers look like shadow-puppet theatre or Greek black-figure
pottery.

- **For a game:** very distinctive and cheap to make, in the spirit of *Limbo*
  or Lotte Reiniger. It ties neatly to Greek black-figure vases, and silhouettes
  animate well.
- **Watch for:** faces and expressions are lost, so this doesn't work for a
  story told through dialogue.

## 8. Films (2010 and 2013)

<img src="images/film2010-poster-percy-annabeth-grover-shield.jpg" width="150" alt="2010 film poster with trio"> <img src="images/film2010-key-art-trio-wave.jpg" width="240" alt="2010 key art, trio in wave"> <img src="images/film2013-still-percy-annabeth-ship.jpg" width="220" alt="2013 still, Percy and Annabeth on deck">

*The Lightning Thief* (2010) and *Sea of Monsters* (2013) starred Logan Lerman
and Alexandra Daddario. The look is glossy blockbuster: teal-and-orange grading,
storm, water and lightning effects, and older-teen leads in jackets and hoodies.
Annabeth is brown-haired in the first film and blonde in the second, in a
hoodie or jacket, with a shield. The Blu-ray-style "blue stylised Percy" poster is the cleanest
graphic piece.

- **For a game:** only useful as mood and effects reference (water and
  lightning) for a realistic 3D game.
- **Watch for:** the films are widely disliked by fans and depart from the
  books.

## 9. Disney+ series (live action)

<img src="images/disney-series-still-percy-annabeth-helmets.jpg" width="230" alt="Percy and Annabeth in bronze helmets"> <img src="images/disney-series-still-camp-half-blood-shirts.jpg" width="230" alt="Trio in camp shirts"> <img src="images/disney-series-still-annabeth-gold-armour.jpg" width="230" alt="Annabeth in armour at camp"> <img src="images/disney-series-s2-key-art-poster.jpg" width="150" alt="Season 2 key art">

The series (2023–) stars Walker Scobell and Leah Sava Jeffries. It is grounded
and earthy: forest greens, camp oranges, hand-built bronze armour with
horsehair plumes (costume designer Tish Monaghan). The leads are book-age
twelve-year-olds. Annabeth has long braids and a serious, watchful face. Her
armour is gold-bronze with a crested helmet.

- **For a game:** the best costume and prop reference: camp shirts, helmets,
  breastplates, shields and Riptide. It is useful whichever style is chosen.
- **Watch for:** basing the look on these specific actors' likenesses is a step
  further than basing it on the books.

## 10. Disney+ gilded key art

<img src="images/disney-series-s1-percy-character-poster.jpg" width="170" alt="Percy character poster"> <img src="images/disney-series-s1-annabeth-character-poster.jpg" width="170" alt="Annabeth character poster"> <img src="images/disney-series-illustrated-poster.jpg" width="170" alt="Illustrated series poster">

The season-1 character posters frame each hero in an ornate black-and-gold
plate. Percy's has tridents and hippocampi. Annabeth's has owls and an olive
motif for Athena. The palette is warm orange-gold leaf on black, like a lit
Greek vase. The illustrated variant poster is flat painted waves and fire in
blue and orange, with a tiny silhouetted Percy.

- **For a game:** a ready-made scheme for character-select cards, ability icons
  and title screens: each hero framed by their parent god's emblems. It pairs
  naturally with family 5 or 7.
- **Watch for:** it frames characters but doesn't say how to draw them.

## 11. Stage musical

<img src="images/musical-broadway-2019-percy-annabeth-grover.jpg" width="230" alt="Broadway production photo"> <img src="images/musical-london-2024-production.jpg" width="230" alt="London 2024 production"> <img src="images/musical-poster.jpg" width="140" alt="Musical key art">

*The Lightning Thief: The Percy Jackson Musical* (first staged 2014, off-Broadway
2017, Broadway 2019, London 2024) is deliberately scrappy. Costumes are thrift-store and DIY:
toilet-paper and leaf-blower monster effects, saturated stage light and
spray-paint graffiti key art in yellow on indigo. Annabeth is in camp orange
with a ponytail.

- **For a game:** an appealing "made by campers" idea: cardboard, tape,
  graffiti and stagey lighting, as in a papercraft or *Tearaway*-style game.
- **Watch for:** few good images exist, and the official production galleries
  wouldn't download.

## 12. DS game (2010)

<img src="images/game-ds2010-box-art-front.jpg" width="170" alt="DS box art"> <img src="images/game-ds2010-screenshot-party-battle-ui.jpg" width="140" alt="DS party battle"> <img src="images/game-ds2010-screenshot-trident-power.jpg" width="140" alt="DS trident power">

The only licensed *Lightning Thief* game, a Nintendo DS tactics RPG (Activision
and Griptonite, 2010), tied to the film. It has a low-poly 3D field on the top
screen and a party of portrait cards with health and power icons on the touch
screen. Percy, Annabeth and Grover fight together and Percy has a trident
special attack.

- **For a game:** worth noting as a design reference (a party of three, each
  with a god-given power) more than as a look.
- **Watch for:** the art is film-likeness and dated.

## 13. Toys

<img src="images/merch-funko-pop-percy-disney-series.jpg" width="180" alt="Funko Pop Percy">

The Funko Pop of the Disney+ Percy has a big head, black button eyes, a camp
shirt and a gold Riptide.

- **For a game:** points to the chibi option. Big heads read well at small
  sizes and on phones, and suit a casual or party game.
- **Watch for:** it's generic Funko styling, not a Percy Jackson look of its
  own.

## Choosing

Three combinations stand out. Each pairs a way of drawing the characters with a
way of dressing the world:

1. **Cartoon heroes in an ornamental world:** family 6 characters, with family 5
   and 10 for UI, maps and backdrops. Readable, animatable and distinctly
   Greek. It's the safest choice for a game for younger players.
2. **Watercolour storybook:** family 1 interiors and family 2 portraits, with
   painted backdrops and few animation frames. It suits a story-led adventure
   or visual novel.
3. **Black-figure shadow play:** family 7 figures over family 5 colour grounds,
   like moving Greek vases. It's the most original and cheapest to make, but
   expressions have to come from pose, not face.

Whichever is chosen, family 9 settles the costumes and props, and the constants
above keep Percy and Annabeth recognisable.
