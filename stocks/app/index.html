    How total return is worked out

    • Price only charts the share price. Prices are adjusted for splits, never for dividends.
    • With dividends buys at the close on the first day of the chart and keeps every dividend as cash (no interest), so the line is shares × price + cash collected.
    • Dividends reinvested uses each dividend to buy more shares at the close on its ex-date, so payments compound in their own rhythm: weekly, monthly or quarterly.
    • A dividend counts when its ex-date falls after the purchase day. Taxes, fees and the delay between ex-date and pay date are ignored.
    • The chart plots daily points for up to a year, weekly up to five years and monthly beyond; the maths always runs on daily closes. A symbol younger than the range charts from its first close, at the detail that span calls for.
