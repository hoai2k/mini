How to play

Bubble Chat puts everyone who opens this page in the same video chat. Pick a name, press Start and allow the camera and microphone.

Face effects

Open Effects and pick one. Everyone sees your effect, because it is drawn on the video you send. Many react to your face: pucker your lips to blow a bubble that floats over and pops on your friend's face, open your mouth for the dragon's fire or the rainbow, smile for hearts or sunglasses, raise your eyebrows to drop the monocle. With no camera face, hold Space (or press and hold your own video) instead.

Games

Open Games and start one. Friends get a Join button. Scores and records are kept on this device.
