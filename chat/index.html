How to play

Games Chat puts everyone who opens this page in the same video chat. Pick a name, press Start and allow the camera and microphone.

Face effects

The bottom row has Favorites, Effects (warps, colour, time tricks), Accessories (hats, ears, horns, masks, hearts, rainbows, tears, snow and bubbles), Backgrounds (new backgrounds and 360 worlds) and Games. Pick one and it slides to the left, with what's on shown beside it; tap it again to turn the effect off and show the whole row. Swipe the strip: whichever stops in the ring is on. Tap ⓘ to see how the one that's on works, the thumbs to rate it (thumbs up adds it to Favorites), and share to offer it to everyone. Tap Use your friend's effect to put their effect on you too: the main video has a labeled button above the bottom row, and small videos have their own button. Effects turn off when you start a game and stay off while you play.

Everyone sees your effect, because it is drawn on the video you send. Everyone sees your effect, because it is drawn on the video you send. Many react to your face: pucker your lips to blow a bubble that floats over and pops on your friend's face, open your mouth for the dragon's fire or the rainbow, smile for hearts or sunglasses, raise your eyebrows to drop the monocle. With no camera face, hold Space (or press and hold your own video) instead.

Games

Open Games and start one. Friends get a Join button. Scores and records are kept on this device.

Sheets and your video

Drag a strip up to see all of it in a grid; pull the grid down to go back to the strip. Drag the bottom controls down to make room: first the button row tucks away, then the strip; drag up (or tap the little grip) to bring them back. Pull the strip, the Games list or Messages down to peek at the video behind them; pull further, or flick, to close. With any number of people, tap a small video or its Main view button to make it the big one; drag each small video wherever you want it. The microphone, camera and settings are at the top right; Messages, this help and Leave are in Settings.

Good to know

On iPhone, web pages can't go full screen: add Games Chat to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there. Calls go straight between browsers. On some strict networks (some offices and mobile carriers) the video can't connect; the chat and games still work.
