This browser didn't let the page go full screen. Add Games Chat to your Home Screen instead and open it from there: it fills the whole screen like an app.

Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.

How to play

Games Chat puts everyone who opens this page in the same video chat. Pick a name, press Start and allow the camera and microphone.

Face effects

Open Effects and swipe the strip: whichever effect stops in the ring is on. Everyone sees your effect, because it is drawn on the video you send. Many react to your face: pucker your lips to blow a bubble that floats over and pops on your friend's face, open your mouth for the dragon's fire or the rainbow, smile for hearts or sunglasses, raise your eyebrows to drop the monocle. With no camera face, hold Space (or press and hold your own video) instead.

Games

Open Games and start one. Friends get a Join button. Scores and records are kept on this device.

Sheets and your video

Drag the Effects strip up to see every effect in a grid; pull the grid down to go back to the strip. Pull the strip, the Games list or Messages down to peek at the video behind them; pull further, or flick, to close. With two people, tap the small video to swap it with the big one, and drag it to any corner.

Good to know

On iPhone, web pages can't go full screen: add Games Chat to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there. Calls go straight between browsers. On some strict networks (some offices and mobile carriers) the video can't connect; the chat and games still work.
