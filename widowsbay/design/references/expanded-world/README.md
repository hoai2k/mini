# Beyond the music video: expanded world references

Nine companion illustrations informed by frames from the full 1903 film. Eight explore subjects or actions beyond the sampled music-video sequence; the armoury is a bonus deeper study of a setting already glimpsed in the edit. These are interpreted worldbuilding references, with invented details identified below.

[Open the gallery](index.html), [view the source film](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Le_Royaume_des_f%C3%A9es_(1903).webm), or read [the complete prompt set](prompts.json). Generated with built-in imagegen, using the cropped browser frames in `source-frames/`. Full browser captures are retained beside them. Times belong to the 16:33 copy and may differ in other releases.

The full film adds an abduction, a forbidden castle vision, the fairy’s arming gesture and an approach across a sea moat to the voyage imagery. The antagonist’s hood and the red/teal stage servants offer useful silhouettes. Heraldic stonework, weapon racks and painted night skies extend the same theatrical visual vocabulary. This does not establish the identities or motives of the song’s sea witch and beloved.

- [Royal betrothal court](betrothal-court.png) — The full film’s opening ceremonial court has a different architectural set from the music video’s ornate bedchamber. Courtier faces, small heraldic marks and theatre framing are reconstructed.
- [Moonlit castle rampart](moonlit-rampart.png) — The nocturnal rampart and aerial-abduction passage are outside the sampled music-video sequence. People are removed to reveal the carved railing, beasts, sky and landscape.
- [Flying chariot of fire](flying-fire-chariot.png) — Inspired by the aerial abduction vehicle and its bat-wing canopy. This is a worldbuilding redesign: the generator combines the carriage with the rampart’s shield-decorated railing. It is not an exact reconstruction of the film vehicle.
- [Royal armoury and vaulted hall](royal-armoury.png) — Bonus deeper setting study. This hall appears briefly in the music video around 0:30–0:35; it is included for expanded environmental coverage, rather than claimed as an omitted setting. Actors are removed and architectural/weapon details are completed.
- [The witch's forbidden realm](witch-prison-vision.png) — Based on the forbidding landscape shown as a magical vision before the sea journey. The sky, tower silhouettes and colour-coded attendants are source-led; tower spacing, foreground placement and some heraldry are invented.
- [Hooded witch costume study](hooded-witch.png) — Study of the hunched, dark-teal-hooded antagonist in the full film. This descriptive role is distinct from the earlier pink magical figure; identification with the song’s sea witch remains unconfirmed. Hidden face, added views and scenic montage are interpretive.
- [Red and teal demon attendants](demon-attendants.png) — The full film’s red and teal costumed servants and flame-bearing action. Facial makeup, headpiece close-ups and some costume ornament are completed from low-resolution footage. These are stage performers, rather than naturalistic monsters.
- [Fairy-given helmet and sword](fairy-gifts.png) — The fairy’s arming gesture before departure. The reference enlarges and elaborates helmet, sword and shield shapes; the detailed designs are adaptations, not certified prop reproductions.
- [Island tower across the sea moat](sea-moat-tower.png) — The tower and separated coastal approach before the rescue. This image adapts the source to a night mood, adding a gold moon and decorative rocky frame; those additions are not present in the captured input.
