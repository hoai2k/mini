How the Play Goes

A toy theatre in five acts. Each act is staged with a different piece of stage machinery; its card tells you what the A button does. Keepsakes you carry from act to act decide how the story ends. Acts you have reached can be replayed from the playbill.

Act I — The Crossing. Sail the painted sea to Widow’s Bay before the sea witch wrecks you. The stagehands crank three rows of waves; hop between them.

  • Rocks rise through trapdoors where the water churns. Leap over them or change rows.
  • Lightning falls on whichever row the witch’s cloud hangs above. Leaping won’t help — change rows.
  • The wind shifts. Watch the vane: a headwind pushes you back and slows the voyage, a tailwind carries you on.
  • Pearls score. Three tea leaves float by once each; together they complete the reading, which warns you of danger in the acts to come.
  • When the bay is in sight the deck becomes a party: press A on the beat to light the lanterns.
  • Three lanterns are your hull. Lose them all and the sea keeps you.
