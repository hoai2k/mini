# Widow's Bay visual references

These are source references, not generated game artwork.

The four `video-*.jpg` files are exact copies of the preview frames already supplied in `design/references/` on main. The original request document identifies them as frames from Langan, Frost & Wane's Widow's Bay video. No additional timestamp or original video URL was available in the repository; these are not claimed to be new captures.

`melies-1903-film-preview.jpg` is Wikimedia Commons' 500-pixel preview still for Georges Méliès, *Le Royaume des fées* (1903), downloaded 2026-10-04. The source page identifies the film as public domain and gives the source as *Les Contes merveilleux de Méliès en couleur*.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Le_Royaume_des_f%C3%A9es_(1903).webm
- Image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1d/Le_Royaume_des_f%C3%A9es_%281903%29.webm/500px--Le_Royaume_des_f%C3%A9es_%281903%29.webm.jpg

Use the video cover for lettering and the trio, the intertitles for palette and type, and the grotto frame for the witch, moonboat, dragon and scene painting. The film preview supplies a further example of front-on scenery and hand tinting. The rest of Part A2's requested video moments require access to the actual band video and remain reference-gathering requests.
