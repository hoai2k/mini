# The witch in the 1903 film

Crops of two frames from the Wikimedia Commons copy of *Le Royaume des fées*
(the full captures are in `../expanded-world/source-frames/`):

- `witch-05m18s.jpg` — in the armoury, bent almost double over her staff in
  her heavy sea-green hooded robe.
- `witch-13m20s.jpg` — struggling with the prince at the cliff edge, her pale
  old face showing under the hood.

She is a living old crone played on stage, not a spectre: one shaggy woollen
robe in full folds to the ground, a cord girdle, bare old hands and a plain
staff. The F7 requests in `../../image-requests.md` attach these.
