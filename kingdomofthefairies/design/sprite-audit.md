# Sprite audit

Generated by `design/source/audit_sprites.py` from the delivered art; rerun it after every delivery. Heights and tops are in art pixels.

## Canvases and feet

Every delivered bottom-centre figure must fill its requested canvas and stand on its bottom edge (at most 3 px of clear canvas under the lowest opaque row; an idle frame, where its still stands).

All pass.

## Walking cycles

Head top per frame, its dip below the passing frames (of the figure height; a walk dips about 2.5%), and painted body size against contact A (head-and-shoulders match; the match score shows how far to trust it). A frame that dips further is scaled up from its feet to a 2.5% dip in play (`src/sprite-fit.js`) and should be redrawn.
