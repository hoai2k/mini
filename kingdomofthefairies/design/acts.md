# Kingdom of the Fairies — five-act design

The current story and first chapters remain as built. The full rescue voyage in Méliès’s 1903 film is a reference for decisions about later updates, not a commitment to replace the existing adaptation.
[Film story and sources](film-story.md) record the original narrative; the source-led
[expanded-world images](references/expanded-world/README.md) and
[new concept sheet](generated/fairies/story-sheet.webp) define visual references.
Keep the toy theatre, painted scenery, front-facing compositions, cut-outs,
visible stage machinery, transformations and hand-tinted print look.

## What is implemented in this pass

The existing five acts and their rules remain playable. The default title is
Kingdom of the Fairies and Act V is The Enchanted Tower. Modern band figures
are removed throughout, including their procedural fallbacks and animation
loads. Scenic moving stones occupy the old cliff footholds at identical anchors.
Only period music plays, including the opening, pageant and curtain call.
The query edition preserves the old presentation and assets.

The sailor, sinking guitar, tea prophecy, rose bargain, interior-whale platform
course, and branching endings remain existing game inventions. Their presence
is not a statement that they appear in the film. New story material below is a
future design brief, not implemented gameplay.

## Character and art rules

Aurora is a benefactor, never a seductive disguise for the hostile witch.
Azurine is a captive princess until rescued; avoid placing her in the whale or
underwater entourage. Bel-Azor is the film’s prince, while the existing sailor
sprite remains a temporary game avatar. The water fairy is a guide; Neptune’s
court supplies passage. Demons perform the kidnapping and fire-setting.
Use theatrical costume silhouettes and deliberate stage gestures, not modern
musicians. Avoid relabelling the existing pink witch sprite as Aurora without
first splitting its gameplay role and art.

Retain existing scenery wherever its subject supports the new direction.
Before replacing any asset, compare its action and costume with the full film.
The [image brief](image-requests.md) tracks new delivery and future gaps.
[The act contract](../src/acts/CONTRACT.md) governs implementation and checkpoints.
The original detailed rules, timing diagrams and old creative direction are
preserved in [legacy/acts.md](legacy/acts.md) for the query edition.
