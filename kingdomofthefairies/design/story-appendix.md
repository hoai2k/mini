# Kingdom of the Fairies — future story updates

Use [the complete film sequence](film-story.md) and [the act mapping](acts.md)
as the baseline. These proposals are not part of this theme-only update.

1. **Royal prologue.** Stage the betrothal, four gifts, insulted witch, ruined
   protective flower, aerial kidnapping and alarm. Deliver a sleeping Azurine,
   four gift props, demons and a fiery chariot; reuse court and rampart studies.
2. **Prison vision and arming.** Dissolve the armoury into the isolated tower;
   Aurora releases Bel-Azor and equips him with enchanted protection. Split
   Aurora and the witch into separate cast slots and roles first.
3. **Water fairy and Neptune.** Convert the undersea journey into a guided
   procession; add conch/dolphin transport, fish mounts, lobster guards and
   floral transformations. Preserve readable hazards and existing swimming.
4. **Whale transport and coastal approach.** The whale is Neptune’s helpful
   transport, not punishment. Its interior can remain an expanded playable
   space. Deliver the party ashore, then show Aurora’s cave route and the
   remaining sea gap before the dive.
5. **Rescue and resolution.** Give torch-bearing demons the fire-setting action;
   stage the stair collapse, rescued Azurine, barrel and whirlpool. Replace
   keepsake-dependent endings only when separately requested.
6. **Wedding and apotheosis.** Use the existing pageant system for procession,
   cloud dissolves, fairy colonnade and crowning. Do not reintroduce a band.

Catalogue-only cavern monsters, boa, waterfall and dancing boots need visual
verification in the selected film copy before implementation. New concepts
should be labelled as adaptations when they extend the film’s events.

Existing interpreted references are in `references/expanded-world/`; the
new full-journey concept sheet is in `generated/fairies/`. Its documented
shortcuts are not continuity instructions.
