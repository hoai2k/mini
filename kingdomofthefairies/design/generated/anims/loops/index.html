Widow’s Bay · eleven motion assets

Three tracked-footage loops and eight painted-puppet animations. No Runway or image-to-video service was used. Painted loops preserve the existing artwork with continuous deformation; they are not independently redrawn frames.

The footage lady is a hand flourish and sway, rather than a full dance. The giant retains the photographic softness of the source video. Each asset’s notes disclose its source and spatial/temporal sampling.

The wand preview replays automatically; in the game it plays once when the spell starts.
