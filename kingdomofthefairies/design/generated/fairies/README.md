The title is game art. The story sheet is documentation art, not loaded by
play. It preserves all story groups but contains illustrative shortcuts:
The whale panel was edited to show only the prince and his party leaving its
mouth; Azurine remains captive elsewhere. The lobster scale is exaggerated, and rescue and barrel
punishment are compressed together. Consult `../../film-story.md` and the
source film before designing from it. Generated images are not historical
stills or exact reconstructions.
