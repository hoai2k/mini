# One Monster After Another: the Adventure

*The Adventure* is the game told as a side-scrolling picture book. The letter
travels left to right across nine long **boards**, one for each snatch in the
book, and on each board you are a different monster, with its own way of
moving, its own hazards and its own prize to collect on the way. Every board
ends in the snatch, as each minigame does (`design.md`), so the story stays
the same: you are always the monster trying to get the letter from whoever
has it.

The minigames stay as they are for *Snatch & Keep* and party play; the
Adventure sits first on the "How shall we play?" screen. It is for one
player, or two to four taking turns a board each, passing the controller
with the letter.

## What every board shares

- **A long scroll.** A world 1080 units high and about 9,000 to 15,000 wide,
  played left to right. The camera leads the monster in the way it is going
  and is clamped to the world's ends. Behind the action, the board's three
  backdrop panels scroll slowly (a far layer); then the ground and
  everything the monster touches; now and then a foreground branch or fern
  passes in front, faster, for depth.
- **One verb per monster.** The same three inputs as the minigames: *move*,
  *Action* and *Sprint*. Action is always the monster's own verb (hop, flap,
  lunge, huff, suck, pound, swing); Sprint is its burst (scurry, hard
  wingbeats, jet, ride the current, storm along, roll, pole-vault). Up and
  down mean what they mean for that monster (duck, dive, sink, rise).
- **Sections.** Each board opens with a safe stretch that teaches the move,
  then three or four sections that each add one new twist (a lookout, a
  gust, a current, a net), a breather, and the climax: the snatch. A
  signpost marks each section, and is also where you come back if you fall
  in a hole or get caught: no lives, no game over, only lost time.
- **The holder runs ahead.** The monster with the letter is on the board,
  usually somewhere ahead, and a little strip at the top of the screen shows
  the route, where you are and where the letter is.
- **Collectibles from the lore.** Each monster gathers what it loves
  (stamps, paper scraps, plain Bombanats for lunch, sea lions, goo, more
  paper, Bombanats for the collection), about 25 to 40 per board, some easy,
  some hidden or risky.
- **A clock.** Each board has a time limit from the story (before the
  mailman comes, before the boat reaches land, before morning). If it runs
  out the letter goes on anyway, as in the book, with no postmarks.
- **Postmarks.** One for the snatch, one for gathering most of the board's
  collectibles, and one for the board's own feat, written on the board's
  card. Gentle mode gives more time and softer hazards; postmarks still
  count.
- **No scares.** Hazards bump, daze, soak, tangle or shoo; nothing dies.

## The boards

### 1. The Stamp-Collecting Trollusk: Faddle Street

*From the wood where he lives, along Faddle Street to Sally Ann's mailbox,
before the mailman comes.* (150 s)

- **Moves.** Walking is a silent tiptoe. *Sprint* scurries: fast, but every
  step makes a noise ring. *Action* hops (hold for higher); a hop that
  reaches a fence top or a ledge grabs it and pulls him up. *Down* ducks:
  in a bush or a flowerbed he is hidden. Standing still for a moment he
  freezes in disguise behind a leafy twig, which fools a lookout from far
  off but not close up.
- **Sections.** (1) The wood's edge: roots, logs and stamps blowing on the
  wind, to learn the hop. (2) The neighbour's garden: she dozes in her
  rocking chair and peers out now and then (a little eye warns first) over
  a stretch of lane; be in cover, or frozen and far off, when she looks.
  (3) The dog's yard: the dog sleeps by its kennel; a scurry near it wakes
  it, and an awake dog runs out to the end of its rope, so get up on the
  fence or a dustbin. (4) The fence run: along the picket fence tops, a
  sprinkler sweeping the lane (a soaking knocks him back), a dustbin lid as
  a springboard to the stamps in the tree. (5) Sally Ann's house: she looks
  out of her window; the mailman's motorcycle is on the road behind,
  getting closer as the clock runs.
- **The snatch.** Reach the mailbox and press Action to pull the letter out.
- **Collect:** stamps. **Feat:** never caught by a lookout or the dog.

### 2. The Letter-Eating Bombanat: Out of Nowhere

*Through the wood after the Trollusk, who runs along the floor below with the
letter.* (120 s)

- **Moves.** Each press of *Action* is a wing beat; between beats he glides
  and sinks slowly. *Down* tucks the wings for a dive that gains speed;
  letting go of down swoops up, trading the speed for height. *Sprint* is
  hard wingbeats, fast, drawing on a stamina ring. Gliding slowly onto a
  branch perches him, which refills it.
- **Sections.** (1) The deep wood: branches and trunks to weave between
  (a bump tumbles him and he stalls). (2) Cobwebs and hanging vines: a web
  holds him until he flaps free. (3) The knot-faced trees, which blow gusts
  when he passes, and the cross red birds that peck. (4) The edge of the
  wood above the goo.
- **The holder.** The Trollusk scurries along the floor, ducking into
  bushes and hollow logs and dropping stamps. In each clearing the canopy
  opens: dive on him while he is in the open to snap up the letter. A snap
  in an early clearing ends the board early.
- **Collect:** paper scraps (tickets, menus, paper hats, newspaper).
  **Feat:** the snap in the first or second clearing.

### 3. The Bombanat-Munching Grumley: A Nice Bombanat Lunch

*Under the Blue Ocean of Bubbley Goo, after the Bombanat flying above.* (120 s)

- **Moves.** Under the goo he swims in any direction, heavy and floaty,
  tentacles paddling. *Sprint* squirts a jet of goo for a dash. *Action*
  lunges a crab claw the way he is pointing; near the surface it shoots up
  out of the goo. Swimming up fast at the surface leaps him right out, in
  a splash.
- **Sections.** (1) The shallows: plain Bombanats skimming low, to learn
  the lunge. (2) The weed forest: scowling fish that nip, a giant eye that
  wakes. (3) The fishing grounds: anchors dropping and nets let down from
  boats above (a net holds him until he wriggles free, mashing Action).
  (4) The whirlpools, which pull down, and the big goo bubbles, which carry
  him up.
- **The holder.** The Bombanat in the striped necktie flies ahead, looking
  for somewhere to land; each lunge that misses him scares him higher for a
  while. At the island at the end he tries to land: grab him.
- **Collect:** plain Bombanats for the lunch basket. **Feat:** never caught
  in a net.

### 4. The Furious-Floating Ice-Ferg: SCRUNNCCCHHH!!

*Across the sea after the fishing boat, before it reaches the harbour.* (120 s)

- **Moves.** Heavy and slow: the stick pushes him along and he keeps his
  momentum. *Up* rises high in the water, *down* sinks low (under a pier or
  a bridge). Holding *Action* huffs up his fury, swelling with freezing
  mist; letting go charges, a surge that smashes crates, floes and barrels.
  *Sprint* rides the current when there is one.
- **Sections.** (1) The open sea: floes to smash, sea lions to pick up.
  (2) The rocks: a charge into rock cracks a chunk off him and throws a sea
  lion off. (3) Warm currents that melt him smaller (faster, but he can't
  smash as much). (4) The pier and the bridge, to sink under, and the
  tugboats that push.
- **The holder.** The *Peg* chugs ahead for the harbour, puffing steam.
  Ram it before it gets there.
- **Collect:** sea lions riding on his ledges (they hop aboard from floes
  and slide off in a crash). **Feat:** reach the *Peg* with eight or more
  sea lions aboard.

### 5. The Wild-'n-Windy Typhoonigator: Suck It All Up

*Across the stormy sky over the goo, after the captain's bottle.* (120 s)

- **Moves.** It rolls freely across the sky; its spout dangles to the sea
  and swings behind it. Holding *Action* sucks up whatever is under the
  spout. Everything it swallows makes it bigger and slower. *Sprint*
  storms along, fast, raining some of it out.
- **Sections.** (1) Open sea: fish, bottles and goo to suck. (2) The
  cliffs and the lighthouse: narrow gaps that only a smaller cloud fits
  through. (3) The windmills, whose sails chop bits of cloud off. (4) The
  sun breaking through: a sunbeam dries it smaller; rain clouds fill it up
  again.
- **The holder.** The captain's bottle (the red-and-white label) bobs ahead,
  and the crew in the pink raft paddle after it with a boat hook. Suck it
  up before they fish it out.
- **Collect:** the goo ocean itself, a meter. **Feat:** suck up nearly all
  of it.

### 6. The Paper-Munching Yalapappus: KER-PLOP

*Through the edge of Nowhere, under the swollen storm cloud letting go.* (120 s)

- **Moves.** A bouncy trundle. *Action* is a big hop; *down* in the air is a
  ground-pound that shakes the trees (paper falls out) and stuns toads.
  *Sprint* rolls him into a ball that bowls through bushes, runs down
  slopes and up ramps, and leaves him dizzy if he rolls too long.
- **Sections.** (1) The jungle edge: paper in the bushes and trees. (2) The
  goo curtains, which stick and slow him. (3) Holes and slopes for rolling.
  (4) The falling things: the cloud overhead lets go of boats, fish and goo
  (their shadows warn), and a boat or a fish on the head dazes him. The
  first little pink octopus that lands on his head stays there, as in the
  book.
- **The snatch.** In the open space at the end, the captain's bottle falls:
  get under its shadow, KER-PLOP, tinkle-plinkle, and mash Action to munch
  the note.
- **Collect:** paper. **Feat:** nothing bonks him on the whole board.

### 7. The Stamp-Collecting Trollusk: Just Passing By

*Creep up on the munching Yalapappus, snatch the letter, and run.* (100 s)

- **Moves.** As on board 1, plus a belly-slide (*down* while running) under
  low branches.
- **Part one, the sneak.** The Yalapappus sits munching; now and then his
  ears prick up and he looks round. Creep from fern to rock, frozen or
  hidden when he looks, and press Action beside him to snatch.
- **Part two, the getaway.** Run right with the Yalapappus trundling after
  you (and rolling, faster, downhill): hop rocks and holes, slide under
  branches, cross the stream on stepping stones. Each bump spills stamps
  from the bag: get them back. If he catches up, a tug: mash Action.
- **Collect:** stamps. **Feat:** never caught in the getaway.

### 8. The Letter-Eating Bombanat: SNIP!

*Out of the sky over open ground, after the running Trollusk.* (90 s)

- **Moves.** As on board 2, but in the open sky: warm air rising off the
  ground lifts him for free, and rain clouds soak his wings heavy.
- **Sections.** Open ground with nowhere for the Trollusk to hide, so it is
  all dodging: the Yalapappus, stomping behind, throws his fork, rocks and
  goo up at the Bombanat. Toward the end, a butterfly net pokes up out of the
  bushes now and then (the Grithix!): it holds him for a moment.
- **The snatch.** Dive on the Trollusk. He dodges a dive he sees coming, so
  come at him fast from high up, or tire him first.
- **Collect:** scraps of the captain's note blowing about. **Feat:** the
  snatch without being hit once.

### 9. The Bombanat-Collecting Grithix: FLIP-FLOP

*Through his wood with his net, and then to the official mailbox.* (150 s)

- **Moves.** Long, unhurried strides. *Action* swings the net in three
  beats, FLIP-FLOP (*up* swings it overhead). *Sprint* while moving plants
  the pole and vaults him over a gap, a log or a monster in a long, slow
  arc; toadstools bounce him.
- **Part one, collecting.** Plain Bombanats flutter through the wood: net
  them for the collection (each one goes in a jar). The Bombanat with the
  letter flits ahead; net it in the clearing.
- **Part two, the post.** Carry the letter to the official mailbox while
  the Trollusk pops out of the ferns to trip him and the Yalapappus rolls at
  him: vault over them, or net them too. Press Action at the mailbox to
  mail it.
- **Collect:** Bombanats. **Feat:** everybody netted before posting.

## How it is built

- `game/src/adventure/index.js`: the boards (titles, texts, time limits,
  monsters) and their loader, as `chapters/index.js` is for the minigames.
- `game/src/adventure/kit.js`: the shared side-scroll kit: the camera, the
  backdrop panels, platforming bodies and solids, the ground stand-ins,
  pickups, particles, the route strip and the hint lines.
- `game/src/adventure/bNN.js`: one board each, keeping the same contract as
  a chapter (`chapters/index.js`), so the shell runs them the same way: the
  card, the count-in, the clock, pause, the celebration and the postmarks.
- Art: each board loads `adv/bNN/backdrop-1..3`, `terrain.<place>`,
  `adv.bNN.props` and `<monster>.moves`, and draws its own stand-in for
  anything not yet delivered (the requests are OM-A, OM-B, OM-G and OM-Y in
  `image-requests.md`).
