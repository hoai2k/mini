      How to play

      The story. Sally Ann posts a letter to her best friend Lucy Jane, and one monster after another snatches it. You play each monster as it snatches the letter from the one that has it: get it, celebrate, and then someone else is eyeing the prize, and you become them.

      Postmarks. Every snatch stamps up to three postmarks on the letter: snatch it fast for more. If time runs out the letter goes on anyway, as in the book, but without a postmark.

      Snatch & Keep. Two players, head to head: one snatches and the other keeps the letter (as the lookouts, the Trollusk, the fishing boat...), swapping sides every chapter. The keeper earns postmarks for holding out. Most postmarks wins.

      Chases. Sprint to close a gap or open one, but sprinting tires you out (the ring at your feet). Hide still in a bush and you can't be seen; moving makes it rustle. A dive or lunge catches a runner, but a well-timed dodge makes it miss. A grab starts a tug: mash Action and pull away. Tired creatures lose tugs, so wear them out first.
