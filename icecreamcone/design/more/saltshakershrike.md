### The Saltshaker Shrike: Just a Pinch (45 s)

The Shrike hangs upside down by its feet from a brass rail over the café
counter, its perforated cap pointing down. Plates slide along the counter
from the kitchen hatch on the right to the diner at the far left. Each has
a little flag with dots: how many pinches of salt it wants (one to five).
Move along the rail and press the action to shake one pinch. The salt
takes about half a second to fall, and the plates keep sliding, so the
trick is to shake a little ahead of a plate. Each pinch that lands fills a
dot on its flag; one too many shows as a red dot. Salt that misses lands on
the counter. The mechanic is counting presses onto a moving target with a
lead, rather than holding to a line (Tea Time) or framing (Say Cheese).

**Scoring.** When a plate reaches the diner: just right 3 ("Just right!"),
one pinch out 1 ("A little bland" or "A little salty", only if it had any
salt), anything else 0 and a frown. Puddings (cake, sundae) carry a flag
with a crossed-out shaker and want no salt: left alone they score nothing,
salted they cost 2.

**Getting harder.** Plates slide at 150 px/s at first and 290 by the end,
and come every 2.8 s at first, every 1.35 s at the end. Orders are one to
three pinches for the first nine seconds, then up to four, then five.
Puddings start after 8 s, about one plate in five. All the plates move
together, so they never overtake.

**Controls.** Controller: left stick or D-pad moves along the rail, A
shakes. Keyboard: ← → or A / D, Space. Mouse or touch: the Shrike follows
the pointer along the rail; click or tap to shake. A faint dotted line
shows where the salt falls. On a narrow screen the view follows the
Shrike, 900 wide.

**Pass.** 30 points. Bots over seeds 1–8: a beginner (leads only 70% as
much as it should, slower, aims loosely, loses count one time in eight)
31–45; a good cook (full lead, keeps pace with the plate) 39–57; shaking
on the spot 2–26; wandering and mashing 4–13; following each plate and
shaking all the way 0–9; idle 0.

**Images.** IC-M14a is the café with its long counter top 70% down and
the wall above kept plain for the falling salt (the game draws its own
brass rail). IC-M14b is the eight dishes on their plates: six savoury, two
puddings. IC-M14c is the four diners, waiting and pulling a face. The
Shrike needs no variant: the game hangs its portrait upside down.
