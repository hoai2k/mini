      How to play

      The field guide. Every page is one of the rare birds from Arnold Lobel’s book. Meet the bird, then play its game: each one is a short challenge, 30 to 60 seconds, doing what that bird’s object does.

      Scores. Each game keeps your best score. Reach the score on its page to open the next bird’s page; beat your best for a New World Record. When every bird has been spotted, they all gather on the last page.

      Every game uses the same few controls: move, and one action button. The card before each game tells you what they do there.
