Scale band 03 · amoebae & ciliates

A garden of large cells.

An amoeba flows, a ciliate reverses, an attached feeder retracts. Their contrasts offer a rich biological vocabulary without inventing magic.

A single cell can be a whole creature.

Amoeba proteus changes outline through broad pseudopods and internal streaming. It can reach roughly 600 µm or more, so an amoeba does not automatically belong “below” a tardigrade in size. The working art reference is a few hundred micrometres across, with a clear edge and granular interior. [S04] · [S06]

Paramecium caudatum supplies a more stable, slipper-shaped body and coordinated ciliary motion. Measured examples in the cited observer’s population are around 200–260 µm long. Vorticella is a smaller attached ciliate with a bell-like body and a contractile stalk: measure the body separately from the whole extended structure. [S07] · [S10]

Three very different kinds of agency.

Food-cup formation, ciliary reversal, local defensive discharge and fast stalk contraction have separate experimental support. They should remain distinct mechanisms instead of cosmetic variants of a dash. [S05] · [S08] · [S09] · [S11]

The substrate is part of the organism’s behavior.

2D possibility

Readable interactions in a thin slice

A brightfield overhead view could make membrane contact, engulfing geometry and attached feeding obvious. A shallow volume or several focal layers would retain the possibility of one cell passing above another.

Substrate-bound amoeboid crawling should read differently from a ciliate swimming through the same field. Repositioning a focal plane is an observation choice, not an ability of the organism.

3D possibility

A transparent, crowded volume

Soft bodies can deform around detritus while ciliates follow curved paths between filaments. Most cytoplasm should remain weakly contrasting; silhouette, internal granules and occlusion can carry form without opaque monster skin.

Detailed cilia may need visual aggregation at distance. The habitat needs background surfaces and particles without filling every cubic micrometre with equally sharp debris.

Concept board / protist garden.

Do not confuse being single-celled with being simple.

Motion, digestion, shape, sensing and osmoregulation happen within one body. A pulsating contractile vacuole can make physiology visible; it is not a heart or a propulsion jet. Binary fission would be a long-timescale biological event, not evidence that a cell can spawn an instant combat clone. Chemical sensing may be represented through a separate explanatory overlay, clearly identified as data rather than visible colored water.
