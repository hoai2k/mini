# Micro

Video games where you play as microscopic creatures.

- [`game/`](game/README.md) is **Micro Zoom**, the game, and all of its
  code and assets: the shell (`game/src/`: title screen, world-select tray,
  intros, the zoom transitions, menu music in `game/music/`), the seven worlds
  it hosts in a frame (`game/springtail/` … `game/phage/`, each also playable
  on its own page), the zoom player (`game/zoom/`), the modules they share
  (`game/shared/`) and the delivered art and sound (`game/assets/`).
  Published at https://hoai2k.github.io/mini/micro/, public (no invite gate);
  the worlds' own pages are at `/micro/game/<world>/`.
- [`text_config.js`](text_config.js) holds every piece of text the player
  sees, in every world and menu ("Editing the game's text" in
  [`game/README.md`](game/README.md)).
- [`game_config.js`](game_config.js) holds the game dynamics worth tuning by
  hand, per world, with their units ("Tuning the game" in
  [`game/README.md`](game/README.md)).

- **The story: the super-gene.** You zoom down the chain, world by world,
  to the bottom: a phage that glows gold. When it gets its genome into a host
  and replicates there, it becomes a super-gene virus, and the game then runs
  back up the chain in reverse (bacterium, Paramecium, amoeba, tardigrade,
  mite, springtail): each creature carries the super-gene, glows gold and is
  far stronger on its board, until a glowing springtail jumps onto a person's
  fingertip and passes the super-gene on. The glow is never explained, only
  shown. On the way up, each world opens with the creature already glowing in
  its golden portal; its boss is the creature from one level up, which it
  beats, bites and merges with, turning it gold, and the view zooms out to play
  that creature next. The golden sparkle on every world's portal is the same gold, so it
  foreshadows the ending. Plan and concept art:
  [`design/super-gene-return.html`](design/super-gene-return.html) (not built
  yet: today the phage world ends in a "Work in progress" screen).
- [`research/`](research/) is a cited research atlas of four scale bands
  (mites, water-film animals, large protists, small cells), their habitats,
  visual styles and real biological "powers". Its pages are built by
  `research/build.py`.
- [`design/`](design/) holds design documents and the image requests
  ([`image-requests.md`](image-requests.md), generated by
  `design/source/build_requests.py`).
- [`design/generated/`](design/generated/index.html) previews the delivered
  artwork, with generation prompts, reference credits and texture-repeat views.
- [`design/zoom-chain-levels.md`](design/zoom-chain-levels.md) specifies the
  Zoom Chain's levels (bog surface, moss canopy, water film, pool, periphyton,
  bacteria, phage) with their enemies and environments, and
  [`design/movement.md`](design/movement.md) each creature's real dash, attack
  and defence. Sound effects are listed in
  [`design/sound-effects.md`](design/sound-effects.md), generated by
  `design/source/build_audio.py` (ElevenLabs; the key stays outside the repo).
- [`prototypes/`](prototypes/) is the prototype browser: a list page linking
  to each world's own page (springtail in macro photo, mite in SEM,
  tardigrade in DIC, amoeba in brightfield, Paramecium in a microscope view,
  bacterium in phase and fluorescence, the phage, and the zoom transitions)
  and a split-screen demo. Published at
  https://hoai2k.github.io/mini/micro/prototypes/ while
  `micro/deploy_config.json` says `includePrototypeBrowser: true` (false
  leaves it out of the deploy; the game and its world pages still deploy).
  The worlds share one control scheme
  ([`prototypes/CONTROLS.md`](prototypes/CONTROLS.md): A dash, X
  attack/eat), a feature panel listing what each does (with techniques
  borrowed from the others, off by default) and sound.
- [`workbench/`](workbench/README.md) is the workbench: its Audio tab plays
  every sound trigger in the game in both sound sets (Default and
  Experimental), the menu sounds and music, and every take with its prompt,
  for marking what to keep, bring into Default or regenerate.
- [`intake/`](intake/README.md) is the drop box for delivered art.
