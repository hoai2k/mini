Micro prototypes

Small experiments towards a game where you play as microscopic creatures. Each tries a different microscope look and a different way of moving. Every one plays with an Xbox controller (or the keyboard).

Springtail

Macro photography

A 1.5 mm water springtail on the bog's surface film: spring off the water with your tail, dodge sundew glue and a rove beetle.

Mite

Scanning electron microscope

An armoured box mite, about 0.6 mm long, walks on eight jointed legs through moss and leaf litter.

Tardigrade

Differential interference contrast

Climb a moss leaf cell by cell with clawed stubby legs while the water film dries around you.

Amoeba

Brightfield

Amoeba proteus: push out pseudopods (each one draws body from elsewhere) and close a food cup around prey.

Paramecium

Darkfield and phase contrast

A fast ciliate in a pond drop: no gliding at this scale, only beating cilia, reversal and escape.

Bacterium

At the limit of the light microscope

Run and tumble: a bacterium can't steer, only choose when to tumble. Follow gradients, dodge grazers and phages.

Phage

Molecular illustration

A virus with no engine: drift, ride the currents, choose the right host and commit. Beyond any light microscope.

Zoom transitions

Every look, world to world

Reach a highlighted spot, then zoom into it: the spot becomes the next world. Try switching the look before, during or after the zoom.
