Sound workbench

Bronze Drum · chapter ambiences and alternate takes, effects, music

Play a chapter's bed and its alternate takes. Each loops until you play another or press Stop all. Match loudness plays every bed at the same level, so you hear the sound, not the volume. Turn it off to hear the level each bed has in the game. Drum along plays a bronze-drum rhythm over the bed at the tempo shown, to hear whether anything in it fights the beat. You can also drum yourself: F is tùng and J is cắc.

Mark each take Keep, Maybe or No and add a note. Your marks stay in this browser. Copy my notes puts them on the clipboard, ready to paste into a message.
