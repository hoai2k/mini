How to drum

Objective. Lead the sisters’ army across the field and destroy the goal: the straw targets in training, the Han fort in battle. You lose if the army’s strength runs out.

Two drummers. Trưng Trắc plays the deep centre of the bronze drum — tùng. Trưng Nhị plays the rim — cắc.

Commands. Each command is a four-beat rhythm. Movement starts on Trắc’s deep stroke (tùng), attacks on Nhị’s rim stroke (cắc). Play it at any speed you like — what counts is that it is even: every beat the same length. A pair (two dots joined underneath) is two quick strokes sharing one beat; a triplet (marked 3) is three. If you stop for too long, or a stroke breaks the rhythm, the phrase fades and you start again. The dots above the drum show the rhythm you are playing: solid for strokes played, faint for those to come.

Stronger rhythms. Every command has three tiers. Tier I is four plain strokes; tiers II and III add pairs and triplets and hit harder (×1.4 and ×1.96). Evenness adds up to a further ×1.4 on top: a sloppy tier II still beats a perfect tier I, and the more even you play, the more it hits.

The army answers. When a rhythm is accepted, the army acts for four beats at your speed while its drums play your rhythm back; your dots stay lit until the drum glows, ready for the next command. The army moves on your strokes, at your speed. A march or a strike carries on in your rhythm for up to two more rounds (weaker) until you start the next command. Nhị’s flourish: while the army answers, rim strokes placed between its beats add power (up to four). Other strokes during the answer are simply ignored.

Shield. Defence is separate from the drum. Han crossbow bolts fly in a visible arc, and a spearman draws back before he lunges. Press shield while an attack is on its way and the army throws a shield wall over the whole group: everything already in the air is deflected (“Defended”). Raise it with nothing coming and it takes longer to be ready again. Shielding never interrupts your rhythm. Made a mistake in a rhythm? Reset it (“Cleared”) and start again.

The drum wakes. Start the next command within two beats of the drum glowing ready to chain. Four in a row and the drum wakes: every command is half again as strong.
