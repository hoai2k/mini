    About

    Photos are read on this device and never uploaded; your catalogue stays here too. Bookzam only sends the words it reads to Open Library, to find which book they are and fetch covers.

    Reading uses PaddleOCR's text models (Apache 2.0), downloaded once (about 25 MB).

    Photos

    Deleting a photo also removes the items only it found.
