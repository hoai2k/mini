Built to fight. Tuned to 174.

Every arena in the circuit has a sound system, because a crowd needs something between rounds. Somewhere around the Jungle Temple residency, five of the fighters noticed that the breaks playing over the ruins were the same tempo as a Fenrir gallop, and that a Konga shoulder salvo lands exactly on the drop.

They started jamming in the loading bay with what they had: Fenrir on the drums, Konga's rocket pods on bass, Cranky's hydro hose as a wet, liquid sample bank, Frogger's sonic croak as the MC, and Nullbot doing whatever Nullbot does to a signal chain. The engineers called it a system fault. The crowd called it the best half-time show in the league.

Now the band writes the music the arenas fight to. Every city gets its own tracks: heavy and jagged for the foundry, glassy and liquid for the quarry, weightless halftime for orbit. The fighters say it makes them better. The band says that was never the point.

The machines

  • Fenrir

    Drums · breaks · The Last Wild Thing

    Four legs, so four limbs on the kit. Plays the amen like it owes him something. Sets the tempo and refuses to discuss it.

  • Konga

    Bass · sub · The Silverback Siege

    The shoulder salvo tuned down to 40 Hz. When Konga drops, the arena floor moves and the lighting rig follows. Keeps the low end honest.

  • Cranky

    Samples · dub · liquid · The Abyssal Bulwark

    Runs the hydro hose through a delay and calls it a sample bank. Responsible for every water sound, every dub chord and the Harbor Docks material. Does not hurry.

  • Frogger

    MC · vocals · The Gunk Gladiator

    The sonic croak is a weapon in the arena and a hook on the record. Hosts the show, hypes the crowd and has never finished a sentence before the drop.

  • Nullbot

    Production · glitch · neurofunk · The Fatal Exception

    A living system error with a laptop. Everything jagged, granular or wrong on purpose is Nullbot. The mix engineer's notes say only: "do not let it near the master bus". It is near the master bus.
