Adult group lessons

Free group lessons are included with all paid memberships. Weather permitting, they run from May 11 until September 1.

Mondays & WednesdaysProgram
6:30 pmAdult Beginner — new to tennis, returning after time away, or building consistency and confidence. Rallying, serving and scoring.
7:15 pmAdult Intermediate — for players who can rally consistently and are comfortable with basic match play. Positioning, point construction and technique.
8:00 pmAdult Clinics ($15 + HST) — higher-intensity live-ball training for intermediate to advanced players. Monday: clinic TBA. Wednesday: Cardio Tennis.

Please sign up before group lessons in the clubhouse. Clinics require sign-up and prepayment before the event. Not sure of your level? Speak with our Club Pro.

Round robins

Meet members, play doubles

A fun way to meet other members and enjoy great doubles tennis in a relaxed atmosphere. Round robins begin the week of May 5. Sign up weekly online or at the clubhouse.

WhenRound robin
Mon, Wed & Fri8:00 – 10:00 amMorning Group Round Robin — a social drop-in where players of all levels play doubles. Coffee and social to follow.
Tuesday7:00 – 9:00 pmLadies' Doubles
Thursday7:00 – 9:00 pmMen's Doubles

Monthly ladders

Develop your skills and individual play on the singles ladder. Men's, ladies' and junior ladders run on a four-week schedule — a great way to meet and compete with players of similar ability. Sign up online or at the clubhouse.

Stratford Tennis League

Collect individual ranking points playing men's, ladies' or mixed doubles. Sign up weekly and the convener sets the matchups by ranking. Sunday evenings, 7:00 – 9:00 pm.

Club tournaments

Super Set days, the regional Stratford Doubles, Masters Doubles and the club championships in singles, doubles and mixed. Draws for every level.

We acknowledge that Stratford is positioned on the traditional territory of the Haudenosaunee, Anishinaabe and the Neutral (Attawandaron) peoples. As we gather, we are reminded that the City of Stratford is situated on treaty land that is steeped in rich Indigenous history and home to many First Nations, Métis, and Inuit peoples today. We are grateful to have the opportunity to live, work, and play on this land.
