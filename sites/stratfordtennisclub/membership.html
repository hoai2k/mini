How to register

Three ways to pay

  1. Online. Register or renew through our booking system, then send payment by e-transfer.
  2. E-transfer. Send payment to membership@stratfordtennisclub.com and clearly note the member's name and membership type in the message.
  3. At the clubhouse. Debit card or cheque, in season, 7:00 am – 10:00 pm daily.

Renewing? You'll need your username and password for the booking system. If you've forgotten them, use the "forgot password" link and they'll be emailed to you.

Court booking & cancellations

Keep the courts moving

  • Courts must be booked in advance using our online system or by contacting the clubhouse.
  • Please don't hold overlapping or excessive reservations that limit court availability for others.
  • Arrive on time. Courts are released if players haven't arrived within 10 minutes of the start time.
  • If you can no longer attend, cancel your booking so someone else can play.
  • All clinics require pre-registration and prepayment; your spot is guaranteed once payment is received.
  • Men's Night, Ladies' Night and many clinics run waitlists. If you can't attend, cancel in advance so the next person can take your place. Repeated no-shows may result in loss of booking privileges.

Code of conduct

Sportsmanship, courtesy and fair play

Members and guests of the Stratford Tennis Club have a duty to encourage and maintain the highest standards of sportsmanship, courtesy and fair play, and to avoid all conduct, acts or practices which are detrimental to the club, its members and guests.

The following are unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

  • Violently, dangerously or with anger hitting, kicking or throwing a tennis ball, racquet or other equipment on the grounds of the club.
  • Verbally or physically abusing other members, guests or staff.

This list is not comprehensive; additional incidents may be deemed unacceptable at the Board's discretion. Violation may result in the loss of membership and/or camp participation, and membership or camp participation in subsequent years may also be denied. Memberships are non-refundable.

The Stratford Tennis Club is released from liability for any incidents that may happen under the supervision of the Stratford Country Club and/or Stratford Youth Soccer during our camps.
