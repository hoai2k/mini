The club

A not-for-profit club run by its members since 1969

Tucked into the scenic Upper Queen's Park beside the Stratford Festival Theatre, the club is a warm, welcoming place for beginners exploring a new way to stay fit and for competitive players chasing a title.

  • Five of Ontario's best clay courts plus a new hard court
  • Practice wall, ball machine rental and equipment to borrow
  • LED lighting for night play
  • Changerooms, lockers and showers
  • On-site racquet stringing

We acknowledge that Stratford is positioned on the traditional territory of the Haudenosaunee, Anishinaabe and the Neutral (Attawandaron) peoples. As we gather, we are reminded that the City of Stratford is situated on treaty land that is steeped in rich Indigenous history and home to many First Nations, Métis, and Inuit peoples today. We are grateful to have the opportunity to live, work, and play on this land.
