Progressive Tennis for Stratford schools

Since 2012 the club has partnered with the Ontario Tennis Association to bring Progressive Tennis — light short racquets, soft oversized balls and smaller nets — to thousands of local students.

Our story with schools

Over 1,500 students and counting

In 2012 the club partnered with the Ontario Tennis Association to provide free demonstrations of Progressive Tennis at Bedford, St. Ambrose, Jeanne Sauvé, St. Joseph's, Anne Hathaway, St. Aloysius, Avon and Hamlet public schools.

Led by OTA Nationally Certified Coach Joel Imamovic, the demonstration days were a huge success with positive feedback from students and educators alike. We're pleased to offer two instructional programs to educators again in 2026, available in May and June.

Email info@stratfordtennisclub.com or call 519-272-0062 (after May 1) with your preferred dates and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Program one

Tennis instruction at your school

Our certified Club Pro conducts the program in your gym with supervision from classroom teachers. All equipment is brought to the school.

  • Ideal for Grades 1 to 6
  • 11:00 am to 3:00 pm
  • $300 for the day
Program two

School visit to the tennis club

Bring the class to us. Our Club Pro runs the program on our hard court and practice wall, with supervision from the classroom teacher. Equipment provided.

  • Ideal for Grades 4 to 8; younger and older classes accommodated
  • 1 or 2 hours
  • $100 for 1 hour · $200 for 2 hours

Bring tennis to your classroom

Dates in May and June go fast. Tell us your preferred days and class sizes and we'll take it from there.

As we gather, we are reminded that the City of Stratford is situated on treaty land that is steeped in rich Indigenous history and home to many First Nations, Métis, and Inuit peoples today.
