Free Kids Tennis, ages 4–12

A free program for members and non-members. Child-size racquets, soft balls and small nets make it easy for young children to rally, play games and build skills gradually.

10:00 – 10:45 amAges 4 – 7
10:45 – 11:30 amAges 8 – 12

No registration or prior experience is required. All equipment is provided by the club.

The club gratefully accepts donations to help purchase and maintain this equipment, so every child has the opportunity to play tennis regardless of membership.

Special event

Optimist Junior Tennis Day

Saturday, June 27 · 10:00 am to noon · Free!

Generously sponsored by the Stratford Optimist Club. All participants ages 4–12 take part in fun tennis games followed by a free lunch and prize table. Members and non-members are welcome. Registration begins at 9:30 am and loaner racquets are available.

We acknowledge that Stratford is positioned on the traditional territory of the Haudenosaunee, Anishinaabe and the Neutral (Attawandaron) peoples. As we gather, we are reminded that the City of Stratford is situated on treaty land that is steeped in rich Indigenous history and home to many First Nations, Métis, and Inuit peoples today. We are grateful to have the opportunity to live, work, and play on this land.
