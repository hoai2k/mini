Adult Beginner · 6:30 pm

New or returning to tennis

For players new to tennis, returning after time away, or looking to build consistency and confidence. Focus on fundamentals: rallying, serving and scoring.

Adult Intermediate · 7:15 pm

Rally, then compete

For players who can rally consistently and are comfortable with basic match play. Focus on consistency, positioning, point construction and technical improvements.

Adult Clinics · 8:00 pm · $15 + HST

Higher intensity, live ball

Designed for intermediate to advanced players looking for higher-intensity, live-ball training and specific instruction. Wednesday's Cardio Tennis pairs drills with a great cardio workout. Not intended for beginners — if you're unsure of your level, speak with the Club Pro.

We acknowledge that Stratford is positioned on the traditional territory of the Haudenosaunee, Anishinaabe and the Neutral (Attawandaron) peoples. As we gather, we are reminded that the City of Stratford is situated on treaty land that is steeped in rich Indigenous history and home to many First Nations, Métis, and Inuit peoples today. We are grateful to have the opportunity to live, work, and play on this land.
