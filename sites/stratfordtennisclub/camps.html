Summer camps

Summer tennis camps, ages 5–14

Register your kids or grandkids for our summer tennis camps. You don't need to be a member. Every camp is offered as a half-day or full-day option in collaboration with golf or soccer.

Register for camps
How camps work

A full week of tennis, with golf or soccer to round out the day

  • Ages 5–14. Groups are organized by age and ability.
  • Half day or full day. Tennis in one half, golf with the Stratford Country Club or soccer with Stratford Youth Soccer in the other.
  • Open to everyone. You do not need to be a member of the club.
  • Equipment provided. Child-size racquets and soft balls are available for younger campers.
  • Coached by our pros and junior coaching staff, on five clay courts and a hard court in Upper Queen's Park.

Registration is handled online through our camp partner's system. Questions? Email info@stratfordtennisclub.com or call 519-272-0062 after May 1.

We acknowledge that Stratford is positioned on the traditional territory of the Haudenosaunee, Anishinaabe and the Neutral (Attawandaron) peoples. As we gather, we are reminded that the City of Stratford is situated on treaty land that is steeped in rich Indigenous history and home to many First Nations, Métis, and Inuit peoples today. We are grateful to have the opportunity to live, work, and play on this land.
