# Langan, Frost & Wane — image requests

The page is complete with the band's own photographs; these would add hand
made art in place of CSS ornaments. Finished files go in `sites/images/`
with the names below, and the page takes web copies in `assets/`. Match the
band's own artwork: late-sixties psych-folk sleeves, the line-drawn
storybook landscape of the 2021 debut cover, the black woodcut of
*Hallowed Child*. Palette: cream paper, brown-black ink, marigold
(#e0b93c), rust (#b4532f), moss (#4d5b2b), plum (#5b1d3e). Never draw the
band members' faces; the photographs do that.

## 1. Vine border

A long horizontal border in brown-black ink on transparent: a single
twisting vine with small leaves, dandelion clocks, a mushroom or two and a
coneflower at intervals, in the style of a 1968 folk-revival songbook. Must
tile left to right seamlessly. Flat ink, no shading.

## 2. Graveyard house

A pen-and-ink drawing on cream paper of an old stone West Philadelphia
twin house with a tiled arched porch hood, potted plants on the steps and
weeds in the path, backing onto a vast overgrown graveyard of leaning
stones, yews and ivy. Gentle and slightly eerie, like an engraving in an
old book of ballads. No people, no text. Light enough to sit behind text at
low opacity.

## 3. Meadowlark

A woodcut of an eastern meadowlark singing on a fence post, wings half
raised, in marigold and brown-black ink on transparent, with a small
sunburst behind it. Bold enough to read at 32 px.
