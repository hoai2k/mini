    About

    Ygent is run by Hoai, who makes small games, as well as the imaginary bands who score them. The games are built using agentic programming, as are the bands. Music is built using Suno.

    Every artist on the label is fictional, but we hope you like their music anyway. It works particularly well in the video games.
