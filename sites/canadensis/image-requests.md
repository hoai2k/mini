## 1. Crest

An engraved ship's-company crest in the manner of an 1880s letterhead: a
fouled anchor crossed with a fiddle and bow, inside a rope ring, the word
CANADENSIS on a ribbon below and "SYDNEY · C.B. · 1887" on a ribbon above.
Single colour (sepia-black ink) on transparent, crisp line engraving, no
shading gradients. Must read at 32 px, so keep the anchor and fiddle bold.

## 2. Portraits

Five studio tintypes in one sheet, five equal panels side by side, each
subject framed head-and-shoulders against a plain dark studio backdrop with a
painted balustrade or a coil of rope at the edge. Same five people as the
deck photograph, same clothes, in this order left to right:

1. Angus MacNeil, fiddle: flat cap, wool coat, holding the fiddle upright.
2. Iain Beaton, whistle: full beard, dark coat, whistle in hand.
3. Duncan Gillis, guitar: full beard, cap, guitar across his knee.
4. Rory MacIsaac, bodhrán: standing, bodhrán under one arm, beater in hand.
5. Màiri Campbell, fiddle: wool shawl, cap, fiddle under her chin.

Tintype look: slightly uneven silver plate, vignetted corners, a hand-tinted
touch of colour on the cheeks only.

## 3. Broadside

A letterpress playbill, portrait, on cream paper with a woodcut of the boat at
the top: "CANADENSIS · Cape Breton Fiddle Music · Aboard the St. Patrick
No. 17 · Toronto Harbour · Thursdays on the Evening Tide · Admission by
the gangway". Heavy wood-type headline, several typefaces stacked in the
Victorian manner, printer's ornaments, an ornamental border. Slight ink
squash and misregistration.

## 4. Chart

An Admiralty-style nautical chart, aged, of the route from Sydney, Cape
Breton, along the Gulf of St. Lawrence, up the river and through the lakes to
Toronto, with a dotted ship's track, a compass rose bottom left, soundings in
the water, and a hand-inked note near Toronto reading "fog lifted here".
Muted blues and sepia; readable place names in a period engraved hand.

## 5. Tavern photograph

Companion to the deck photograph: the same five, same costume, playing in a
low-ceilinged tavern or the boat's cabin by lantern light, a few listeners in
1880s working clothes, one of them holding a modern phone up to record,
treated with the same sepia print look. Landscape, matching the delivered
image's proportions.

## 6. Paper texture

A tileable square of aged cream paper with faint foxing spots, fibres and a
light linen weave, no visible seams, no writing. Neutral enough to sit under
black text.
